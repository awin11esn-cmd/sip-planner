Mutual fund, not ETF, on Fyers

Fyers sells direct mutual funds with zero commission and automatic SIPs. ETFs cost ₹20 or 0.3% brokerage plus GST on every monthly buy and ₹12.5 + GST per scrip when you sell, and factor ETFs trade thinly.

  1. Open Mutual Funds in the Fyers app and search each fund chosen above. Pick Direct – Growth.
  2. Start a monthly SIP for each with the amount shown, all on the same date, and set up the bank autopay once.
  3. Each January, enter your holdings in Drift and rebalancing. If drift is over 5 points, use the "Next SIP split" for a few months instead of selling.
Fund NAVs, costs and 1-year returns come from public fund listings and refresh once each weekday evening; check the Fyers app for the figure you transact at. Index data: NSE Indices (Index Dashboard, factsheets, total-return series). Backtests assume annual rebalancing, no taxes. This is a planning tool, not investment advice.
